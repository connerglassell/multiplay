Common questions

How do I remove the ads?
Open Settings (the gear on the Games tab) and tap Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It belongs to your Apple ID and works on your other devices.
I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads, or I got a new phone.
Open Settings, go to the Ads section and tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.
Can I change or reset the ad consent choice?
Where the law asks for it, Settings > Ads > Privacy Choices reopens the consent form so you can change your answer.
My player names didn't carry over to my other iPhone.
Names, who's playing and your game settings sync through your private iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on for the app on both devices. The scoreboard stays on each device by design.
How do I reset the scoreboard?
Settings > Scoreboard > Reset Scoreboard.
