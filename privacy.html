Multiplay

Pass the Phone: games for two to four players
HomeSupportPrivacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Multiplay is a game app for one phone shared by two to four people. It has no accounts, and the developer runs no server that receives your data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, so Google receives some information as described below.

What stays on your device

Player names, who is playing, your settings and the scoreboard are stored on your device. The app never sends them to the developer.

Settings sync

Your preferences (player names, who is playing, haptics and the pass-the-phone screen) sync between your own devices through your private iCloud account. Nothing that identifies you is stored there, and the scoreboard is not synced.

Ads

Multiplay shows a short full-screen ad between games, provided by Google AdMob. To choose, deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's advertising software may collect and use information from your device, such as your IP address and approximate location, device and app identifiers, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data. Ad content is limited to a general audience. The app does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not use the advertising identifier for tracking. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before ads are set up, and you can change your answer any time in Settings > Ads > Privacy Choices. You can also limit ad personalisation in your iPhone's Settings.

Remove Ads purchase

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details or personal information. Once purchased, the app does not show ads or ask for ad consent.

What the app does not do

It has no accounts, does not use precise location, does not build a profile of you, and uses no analytics service of its own.

Children

Multiplay is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The developer collects no personal information from anyone. Ads are limited to a general audience.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new date. Questions: please open an issue or see the support page.
