Multiplay

Pass and Play: games for two to four players
HomeSupportPrivacy Policy

Multiplay is a set of simple games for two to four people sharing one iPhone. Pick a game, choose who's playing, and pass the phone back and forth. It started with a phone passed around over breadsticks at Olive Garden, waiting for the food.

Four games, one scoreboard.
Tic Tac Toe, Memory Match, Four in a Row and Dots and Boxes, each with bigger boards when three or four people play. A running scoreboard keeps the tally of wins.
No account, no tracking.
Multiplay has no sign-in. A short ad from Google AdMob appears between games, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase turns the ads off for good. Your player names and settings sync only through your own private iCloud account.
